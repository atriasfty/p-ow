# Refund Policy

**Last Updated: May 16, 2026**

### 1. No Refunds

Project Overwatch ("POW", "we", "us", or "our") does **not** offer refunds on any purchases, subscriptions, or recurring charges. All payments are final and non-refundable.

This applies to, without limitation:

* Monthly and annual subscription fees.
* Partial billing periods following cancellation, downgrade, or termination.
* Unused features, seats, or capacity within a paid plan.
* Add-ons and any one-time charges.

### 2. Cancelling Your Subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation prevents future charges; however, you will retain access to paid features until the end of your current billing period. **No prorated or partial refund will be issued** for the remaining time in that period.

### 3. Downgrades

Downgrading from a paid plan to a lower tier (or to the free tier) takes effect at the end of your current billing period. The difference between plan prices is not refunded.

### 4. Failed or Disputed Payments

If a charge appears to have been made in error, please contact us **before** initiating a chargeback or dispute through your payment provider so we can investigate. Accounts associated with fraudulent chargebacks may be suspended or terminated in accordance with our [Terms of Service](legal-terms-of-service.md).

### 5. Non-Profit Operation

Project Overwatch is operated by **Atria**, which is fiscally sponsored by **The Hack Foundation** (d.b.a. Hack Club), a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization (EIN: 81-2908499). Subscription revenue directly funds the continued operation, hosting, and development of the platform.

### 6. Exceptions Required by Law

Nothing in this policy is intended to override consumer protection rights that cannot be waived under the laws applicable to you. Where local law requires a refund in specific circumstances, we will comply with that requirement to the extent legally mandated.

### 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the dashboard or via email. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

### 8. Contact

For billing questions, email **help@atriasafety.org**.
