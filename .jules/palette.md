## 2024-05-30 - Focus states for interactive elements\n**Learning:** Buttons in custom dialogs and modals frequently lack explicit focus visible styles (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`), particularly close buttons (which need offset styles for dark mode) and primary action buttons.\n**Action:** Add comprehensive `focus-visible` styling to all interactive elements, especially icon-only buttons like those for 'refresh' or 'close' and primary actions, to improve keyboard navigation.
