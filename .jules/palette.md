## 2024-05-09 - Ensure Modals have Accessible Close Buttons & Actions
**Learning:** In custom modal implementations (like `dialog-provider.tsx` and `super-server-edit-modal.tsx`), absolute-positioned or icon-only close buttons often lack explicit `aria-label`, `title`, and `focus-visible:ring-2` styles, leading to poor keyboard navigability and unclear intent for screen reader users. Also, standard buttons inside custom Modals need explicit `focus-visible` offset styles to appear clearly against the dark modal background.
**Action:** When creating or reviewing custom modals and alert dialogs, ensure all interactive elements (close icons, cancel buttons, primary action buttons) have descriptive `aria-label`s, tooltip titles, and explicit `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2` classes with appropriate focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-offset-2` if inside a dark modal).
