## 2026-04-29 - Modal Close Button Accessibility\n**Learning:** Icon-only modal close buttons (like the `X` icon in modals) require explicit ARIA labels and focus-visible rings for keyboard accessibility. Many custom modals in the dashboard lack these attributes.\n**Action:** Always add `aria-label="Close modal"` and `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-zinc-400` (or a similar appropriate color) to icon-only close buttons.
