## 2025-01-16 - Custom Dialog Accessibility
**Learning:** Custom dialog/modal containers in this app often lack semantic roles (role="dialog", aria-modal="true") and proper label associations (aria-labelledby). Additionally, icon-only utility buttons within them lack aria-labels, titles, and explicit focus-visible styling with dark offset colors (e.g., focus-visible:ring-offset-zinc-900) necessary for clear keyboard navigation against dark backgrounds.
**Action:** Always add `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby` to modal containers. For icon-only buttons, always include descriptive `aria-label`, `title`, and explicit `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-zinc-900` styling. Include `aria-hidden="true"` on decorative icons.
