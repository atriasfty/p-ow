## 2024-05-08 - Added accessible tooltips and focus styles to map and call modal controls
**Learning:** Found multiple icon-only action buttons (Zoom, Close) in map and call modals missing ARIA labels, title tooltips, and explicit focus-visible states, reducing accessibility for keyboard and screen reader users.
**Action:** Applied aria-labels, title attributes, and explicit focus-visible styles to these icon-only buttons to ensure clear keyboard navigability and semantic meaning.
